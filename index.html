Clear rules

What decides your cashback.

Tracking

The store links your order to CredPoint only when you arrive through our offer button and complete the purchase in that visit.

Eligibility & approval

Orders must meet the offer's conditions. Stores approve them after their return window. Returned or cancelled orders don't qualify.

Withdrawal

Approved cashback is paid to your UPI ID or bank account once your total reaches ₹250. There is no fee.
