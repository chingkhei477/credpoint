Cashback, held to a clear standard.

CredPoint is an Indian cashback and rewards website that shares the commission stores pay us with the people who shop.

When a store pays for sending you its way, part of that should come back to you. CredPoint makes that simple, visible and fair.

What we do

We list cashback offers from partner stores with their rates and conditions in full. When you shop through a CredPoint link, the store pays us a commission. Once the store approves your order, we pay your share to your UPI ID or bank account.

How we earn

Our income is the commission stores pay us. We keep part of it to run CredPoint and pass the rest to you as cashback. You never pay us.

Principles

How we work.

Transparent

Every condition is visible on the offer before you click, and our policies are one tap away on every page.

Secure

We ask only for what's needed to pay you, never for PINs, OTPs, passwords or card details, and we don't sell your data.

Accountable

Questions and complaints go to a real inbox. If a store declines your cashback, we share the reason it gives us.

Business details

Get in touch.

Write to us any time. Our policies explain how cashback, your data, refunds and cancellations are handled.
